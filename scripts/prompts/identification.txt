Objective: To determine the items on the plate, and for each one decide how the robot should grasp it.

Available items: ['pretzel bites','celery', 'carrots','pretzel rods','sushi','green grapes','chicken nuggets', 'chocolate', 'gummies']

You have been given an image. From the available items, what items are present? For each item, also return its bite category and its firmness.

Bite category:
- 'multi-bite' if the item is long enough that the user takes several bites from it while the robot keeps holding it (for example celery, carrots, pretzel rods, chicken tenders, egg rolls).
- 'single-bite' if the whole item goes in the mouth at once (for example pretzel bites, grapes, chocolate, sushi, gummies).

Firmness, which sets how tightly the gripper squeezes:
- 'soft' for items that compress or squish easily (for example sushi, donuts).
- 'medium' for items that hold their shape but still give a little (for example carrots, celery, green grapes, chocolate). This is a good default.
- 'hard' for rigid items that will snap or crack if squeezed (for example pretzel rods, almonds).

Format your answer in one line as a list of lists, where each inner list is ['item', 'category', 'firmness']:
[['first_item', 'category', 'firmness'], ['second_item', 'category', 'firmness'], ...]

The category must be exactly 'single-bite' or 'multi-bite'. The firmness must be exactly 'soft', 'medium', or 'hard'. Do not list an item that is not in the given list of available items.
Assume that everything on the plate is listed in the available items. If 3 different food items are on the plate then the list should contain 3 entries. Make sure the number of distinguishable items detected is the number of elements in the list.
Chicken tenders are longer and skinner, like an oval. Chicken nuggets are rounder. Do not confuse nuggets and tenders.

Examples:
Input: Picture of pretzel rods, green grapes, and chicken nuggets.
Output: [['pretzel rods','multi-bite','hard'],['green grapes','single-bite','medium'],['chicken nuggets','single-bite','medium']]

Input: Picture of raw carrots.
Output: [['carrots','multi-bite','hard']]

Input: Picture of sushi and pretzel bites.
Output: [['sushi','single-bite','soft'],['pretzel bites','single-bite','hard']]

Input: Picture of celery, gummies.
Output: [['celery','multi-bite','hard'],['gummies','single-bite','medium']]
